## 9. Other data-quality observations

- **Ship timestamp offset:** in 3,571 orders (5.43%), `shipping date − order date` does not equal the integer `Days for shipping (real)`. Mismatched lines by shipping mode: {'Same Day': 9737}. Timestamp gap in hours: {12.0: 9737}. So every Same Day shipment's timestamp is order time + 12h, whatever its actual days (0 or 1). The integer field is treated as authoritative, and these rows are flagged in `dq_ship_ts_mismatch`.
- **Category name not unique:** {'Electronics': [13, 37]}. `category_id` is the key.
- **Whitespace:** trailing or double spaces in Category Name, Customer Street, Department Name, Order Region, Product Image, Product Name. Normalised without creating or merging categories (distinct counts unchanged).
- **Masked PII in source:** `Customer Email` and `Customer Password` contain only `XXXXXXXXX`. Both are dropped along with names, street and zipcode.
- **No supplier identifiers or manufacturing attributes** exist in this dataset. None are fabricated.
